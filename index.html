Good to know

Questions before you grow?

The useful stuff, without making you read a 20-page manual.

Do I need a garden?
No. The kits are designed for indoor growing, so a suitable windowsill, shelf or kitchen counter can work.
How long does it take?
Microgreens are fast-growing, but the exact time depends on the variety and growing conditions. Follow the guide included with your kit for the specific variety.
What is included in the kits?
Each kit is different. The comparison above shows the core components included with the Seed Jar, Small, Medium and Large options.
Are your products plastic-free?
Yes. TinyHarvest is designed to be plastic-free, including the packaging approach we use for the products.
Can I buy seeds separately?
Yes. The Seed Jar is intended for customers who already have a suitable growing container or who want to choose their own setup.
