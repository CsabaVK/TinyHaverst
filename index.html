Simple Process

As Easy as 1, 2, 3

No experience needed. Our kit comes with everything you need to grow beautiful microgreens at home.

1

Fill the Tray

Add the included organic growing medium to your tray. Spread it evenly for the best results.

2

Sow the Seeds

Spread the premium seeds evenly across the surface and give them a gentle mist with water.

3

Watch & Harvest

In just 7-10 days your microgreens are ready. Snip just above the soil and enjoy.
