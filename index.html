The TinyHarvest idea

Make growing feel simple.

Growing food at home does not have to mean a garden, complicated equipment, or a new hobby to master. TinyHarvest is built around one clear idea: make the act of growing small, visual and easy to return to.

We focus on the kit, the ritual and the result — not on making the experience feel technical.

The Seed Jar is the smallest starting point. The Small Kit is a better choice when you want a little more growing room without a larger setup. The Medium and Large Kits are for people who want more capacity from the start.
